Frequently Asked Questions

How do you verify authenticity?

Every timepiece undergoes a comprehensive multi-point authentication process including movement inspection, case analysis, dial examination, and provenance verification. We provide detailed certification documents with each purchase.

Do you offer international shipping?

Yes, we ship globally with fully insured packaging. International orders come with complete documentation and international warranty considerations. Contact us for specific shipping details to your location.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 14-day inspection period for all purchases. If you're not completely satisfied, we accept returns in original condition for a full refund or exchange.

Do you provide maintenance services?

Yes, we offer professional servicing, restoration, and maintenance for all luxury watches. Our certified technicians ensure your timepiece remains in pristine condition.

Are payment plans available?

We offer flexible payment options for qualified buyers including installment plans. Contact us directly to discuss financing options for your desired piece.
